## How a heat pump works (plain English)
Instead of using an energy-hungry element to heat water directly, a heat pump draws warmth from the surrounding air and transfers it into your water tank — a bit like a fridge running in reverse. Because it *moves* heat rather than *creating* it, it delivers the same hot water using a fraction of the electricity.
